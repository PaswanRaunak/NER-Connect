Mission Architecture

How It Works: Built for Resilient Terrain

Engineered specifically for seismically active, cloudburst-prone geographic corridors where conventional connectivity fails.

01

Report & Multi-Sensor Ingest

Aggregates raw telemetry from citizen ground reports, BRO earth-moving telemetry, PWD highway sensors, and satellite imagery. Offline mountain areas relay automatically via SMS to 56161.

sensors Ground-truth triangulation active
02

Predict & Risk Classification

AI neural slope-stability models cross-reference IMD rainfall thresholds and slope moisture gradients to anticipate rockfalls 4 to 8 hours prior to catastrophic corridor severance.

psychology IMD Doppler Radar Cross-Sync
03

Prioritize & Dynamic Reroute

Critical life-saving medical supplies and essential fuel receive prioritized corridor clearance, triggering automated green waves and highland bypass routes for registered freight.
